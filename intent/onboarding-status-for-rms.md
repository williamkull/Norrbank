# Intent: onboarding status for relationship managers

Author: M. Lindqvist (client onboarding operations)
Status: accepted
Jira: ONB-2140

## Problem

Corporate clients ask their relationship manager where their onboarding stands, and the RM
rings us. Operations spends roughly a third of inbound time on status-only queries.

## Proposed outcome

RMs see case stage, next step and expected date in their workspace.

## Affected users and systems

Onboarding operations, RM workspace team, onboarding-core, screening-batch, registry-link.

## Constraints

No new personal data in the workspace session. Existing SSO only.

## Open questions

Does the client's treasury contact need to see it in the corporate portal too?
